One leaf hangs from a tree.

Through spring, summer, and autumn, the world around it changes. The wind grows stronger, the seasons pass, and eventually the leaf must face something it has spent the entire game trying to avoid.

A short narrative game about change, attachment, and letting go.

Features

 A short atmospheric narrative experience
 Wind-based gameplay where you protect and stabilize the leaf
Changing seasons and environmental conditions
Original music and an emotional final sequence
Hand-crafted pixel art
Designed to be completed in one sitting
Controls

Arrow Keys — Respond to the wind



Playtime

Approximately: 5 minutes.
